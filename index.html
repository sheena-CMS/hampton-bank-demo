The Change Partner

The Change Partner keeps your change management in sync, at every moment.

Every meeting, email and document goes into The Change Partner. Every plan, register and schedule updates to match, so the people running the change always work from the same facts. It follows the eight steps below, in order.

The method

Eight steps, in order

  1. Ingest the new input
  2. Extract facts, decisions and questions
  3. Check against every artefact
  4. List what changes
  5. Update the artefacts
  6. Update the changelog and actions
  7. Delete answered questions
  8. Run the checks
What goes inMeeting transcripts, emails, process documents and the vendor's public documentation.
What comes outTwenty-two outputs, from the change plan to the weekly status report, each with a version line.
What keeps it rightOne shared facts file, a changelog, and a check that runs before anything is shared.

Overview

Project Compass at Hampton Bank

Project Compass replaces an old CRM with Salesforce plus an AI assistant for about 1,250 people at Hampton Bank, in two waves: Victoria first, then NSW. Every page is a working output of the method, and each one agrees with the others.

Orientation

The project in one minute

About 1,250people in scope: contact centre, branches, business banking and head office
Mon 19 Apr 2027Wave 1 go-live, Victoria (808 people)
Mon 7 Jun 2027Wave 2 go-live, NSW (445 people)
Tue 22 Jun 2027Handover to business as usual

Testing (UAT) runs from Mon 8 Feb to Fri 12 Mar 2027. Victorian training runs from Tue 30 Mar to Fri 16 Apr, and NSW training from Mon 17 May to Fri 4 Jun. Each wave has two weeks of hypercare after go-live. The AI assistant is called Compass Assist and needs sign-off from Risk and Compliance before it is used.

School holidays: go-live on Mon 19 Apr 2027 is clear, but the first two weeks of Victorian training fall inside the school holidays and Easter, so the training schedule is built around leave and shift cover.

Hampton Bank is a fictional company. Demo by Change Made Simple.
