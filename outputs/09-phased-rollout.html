CRM platform team (30) and L&D (8) take over from the project and Northgate Digital.

The gate for every phase

A phase goes live when its scripts pass in the live system, its guide and training are ready, its data is in, and the phase before it has been stable for a week. The 16 criteria on the go / no-go checklist all need a tick. Helen Marchetti signs on Daniel Nguyen's recommendation, and Fatima Haddad signs the Compass Assist guardrails by Fri 9 Apr Updated v0.2.
