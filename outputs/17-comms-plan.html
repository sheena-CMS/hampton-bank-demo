Out of scope: a separate all-staff email, printed flyers and a branch activation desk. Messages go through channels the bank already runs.

Trap: the Victorian school holidays (Fri 26 Mar to Sun 11 Apr 2027) and Easter (Good Friday Fri 26 Mar, Easter Monday Mon 29 Mar) fall across the first two weeks of Victoria training. No drop-ins or leader comms run in that fortnight. Checked against Victorian school term dates and holidays and Victorian public holidays 2027.

Questions to settle

Six questions are open: Q-01, Q-02, Q-03, Q-04, Q-06 and Q-07. Q-05 is answered (stream 1). See the open questions register and the action list.

  • Q-02, prompt changes after go-live: the rules page must say who a leader tells when a draft is wrong.
  • Q-04, complaints queue: the contact centre message and training content depend on it.
  • Q-03, branch training location: roster reminders and booking text differ for each answer.
  • Q-01, legacy CRM data: the sponsor note and FAQ wait for it.
  • Q-07, bankers' client notes: the banker message promises a pre-load that may not include notes.
  • Q-06, NSW trainers: NSW booking opens Mon 3 May only if trainers are named.
