Impact ratings

3 High: the work itself changes, with different steps, different tools or a different job, and a large effort across many units. 2 Medium: moderate change, or new capabilities added to existing work. 1 Low: minor change. 0 None: no change. Five dimensions: how work is done, tools and systems, roles and responsibilities, skills and knowledge, culture and behaviour.
