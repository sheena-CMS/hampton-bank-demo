Q-01

Which Hamlink data comes across to Salesforce?

Decision needed Not in the sources

Owner: CRM platform team and Implementation partner (Jorge Alvarez). Raised in: Baseline, T1. Answer wanted by: Fri 5 Feb 2027.

Context

The programme has agreed to replace Hamlink, but no document lists which record types, how many years of history and which fields move. The data migration risk (R-06) is rated High because of this.

What a good answer looks like

A signed list of record types, fields and cut-off dates, with a reason for anything left behind and a plan for read-only access to the rest.

Worth knowing

Pre-loading each business banker's pipeline (A-25) depends on this list for the banking records.

Affects

  • As-is, to-be and impact: all processes
  • Solution overview: process 1
  • Risk register (R-06)

Q-07

Can business bankers' client notes be loaded from their spreadsheets? Added v0.2 from T4

Decision needed Added in v0.2 Not in the sources

Owner: Business banking and CRM platform team (Richard Vance and Anika Rao). Raised in: Added v0.2, T4. Answer wanted by: Not yet set.

Context

Bankers keep client notes in free-text cells. Loading the pipeline is agreed (A-25). Whether the notes load as well, and in what shape, is not.

What a good answer looks like

A yes or no per note type, a field in Salesforce that holds it, and a test on three real spreadsheets before the pre-load starts.

Worth knowing

Richard Vance has said bankers will use Salesforce only if the pipeline view is better on day one, so missing notes would count against that.

Affects

Q-04

Do complaints stay in the same queue as general enquiries?

Decision needed Not in the sources

Owner: Contact centre and Complaints and advocacy (Tom Gallagher and Louise Chen). Raised in: Baseline, T2. Answer wanted by: Not yet set.

Context

Today an agent who takes a complaint on the phone logs a note in Hamlink and forwards it by email. Service Cloud can route complaint cases to their own queue or keep them in the general queue with a flag.

What a good answer looks like

One routing rule that says where a complaint goes in the first hour, who owns it, and when the regulated clock starts.

Worth knowing

The complaint timers (R-08) start from the routing decision.

Q-02

Who owns changes to Compass Assist prompts and templates after go-live?

Decision needed Not in the sources

Owner: Risk and compliance and CRM platform team (Fatima Haddad and Anika Rao). Raised in: Baseline, T3. Answer wanted by: Not yet set.

Context

Fatima Haddad's team approves the guardrails before go-live. Nothing says who may change a prompt or a template afterwards, or who approves that change.

What a good answer looks like

A named owner for prompt edits, a named approver, and a rule that every change is tested against the approved test set before it goes live.

Risk R-12 sits behind this question. The training demo uses the approved guardrails only.

Q-03

Are branch staff trained at their branch or at a central site?

Schedule risk Inferred

Owner: Branch network (Aroha Williams). Raised in: Baseline, T1. Answer wanted by: Not yet set.

Context

The branch network has 60 branches and 600 staff. Training at branch keeps cover simple but needs equipment and a quiet room. Central sites reduce trainer travel but take people off the floor.

A decision per region (metro and regional) with the room, equipment and cover each option needs.

Worth knowing

The first branch block in the week of 12 April (C-4) is planned for people on leave, so it can run centrally.

Affects
