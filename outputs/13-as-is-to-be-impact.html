Project Compass

As-is, to-be and impact

Draft v0.2 | 29 January 2027 | Change lead, Change Made Simple

Five processes compared stage by stage: how the work runs today, how it runs in Salesforce and Compass Assist, and how much the change affects the people who do it.

Changed in v0.2 5 updates
  • Process 5 now states the Q-05 answer as fact: Compass Assist may draft written complaint responses, the complaints officer must edit and send, and drafts are labelled. Updated v0.2 from T5
  • The guardrails sign-off gate moves from Fri 26 Mar to Fri 9 Apr 2027 (change C-3, process 5 stage 5.4). Updated v0.2 from T5
  • Business bankers have Compass Assist off at go-live and can opt in during week 3 (change C-4, process 3 stage 3.4 and process 5 stage 5.0). Updated v0.2 from T4
  • Process 3 gains a stage for pre-loading each banker's spreadsheet, and Q-07 is added to the open questions. Updated v0.2 from T4
  • Complaint category mapping moves from Fri 29 Jan to Fri 5 Feb 2027 (process 4 stage 4.1).

Grey is how the work runs today in Hamlink and by hand. Green is how it runs in Salesforce. The chip beside each process is the change impact rating: the highest rating of any stage, with the number of High stages beside it.
