What stands out

  1. The Hamlink guide is still the first CRM help a staff member finds, and it describes screens that are being retired.

Where the Compass Assist rules would live

One page on the Hub, Using Compass Assist, under Customer service and linked from the home page. Risk and compliance own it and set a review date.

  • It may suggest answers, draft case notes and draft written complaint responses. The complaints officer must edit and send, and drafts are labelled. Updated v0.2 from T5

  • The guardrails sign-off gate is Fri 9 Apr 2027, moved from Fri 26 Mar. Updated v0.2 from T5

The rules also appear in a banner inside Salesforce and in the role one-page guides. Background reading: Trailhead: meet the Einstein Trust Layer and Trailhead: promote responsible and ethical agents.

Options for a new site

Option A is preferred. The lean version borrows the signpost idea from option C.

Option A: task-first

Best for staff who arrive with a job to do. Short paths to common tasks, and a task tile links straight to a job page. Effort: medium.

Preferred

Option B: process-first

Best for managers who think in the five processes. Frontline staff must know the process name first. Effort: medium to high.

Option C: slim signpost

One page with six links, the lowest effort, but the library keeps its duplicates. Good for Wave 1 as a first step.

What comes off the site

  • The Hamlink user guide (142 pages). Job pages and in-app help replace it, with a page-has-moved link.
  • About 35 of the 41 branch paper forms. Guided onboarding replaces them.
  • The referral email template. The referral record replaces it.
  • The pipeline template. The shared pipeline replaces it and existing copies are loaded before training.
  • The training sign-up tile. The nomination form and the training schedule replace it.

Build guide

  1. List every tile and document, and delete the duplicates on the retire list.
  2. Set up the site navigation for the chosen option.
  3. Build the Using Compass Assist page and review it with Risk and compliance.
  4. Build the job-page tiles for contact centre, branch, business banking and complaints.
  5. Check access for each role, then switch the Hamlink tile to a page-has-moved link on the go-live date.

Use Quick links for task tiles, a text web part for the five rules, and a document library with Owner and Review date columns. Link to files, not sharing links, and keep pages to standard web parts.

Trap: Do not copy text from the Salesforce documentation onto the Hub. Link to the page, because Salesforce changes its wording each release and a copy goes out of date.
