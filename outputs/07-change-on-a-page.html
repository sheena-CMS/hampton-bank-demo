Project Compass: change on a page

Salesforce and Compass Assist | Wave 1 Victoria, Wave 2 New South Wales | Draft v0.2, 29 January 2027

Change level: Complex
Change management strategy

Know what changes in your day, practise it before go-live, and have someone on your floor to ask in the first two weeks.

StaffUnderstand what changes in their work and when, and get training that fits their shift.
BankersUse one shared pipeline that is already loaded when they first log in.
ComplianceApprove Compass Assist, and a person reviews every draft.
1VisionA visible customer-experience win by June, measured on six themes.
2AnalysisFive processes change. 20 of 25 stages are rated High.
3PlanA plan per team: impact, listening, engagement, messages, capability.
4NetworkThree banker champions named. NSW champions to come.
5ExecuteTwo go-lives, each with two weeks of hypercare on the floor.

Milestones

  1. Mon 8 Feb: UAT starts. Wed 17 Mar: UAT sign-off.
  2. Fri 26 Mar to Sun 11 Apr: school holidays and Easter.
  3. Tue 30 Mar: Wave 1 training starts. Fri 9 Apr: Compass Assist gate.
  4. Mon 19 Apr: Wave 1 go-live, Victoria.
  5. Mon 17 May: Wave 2 training starts. Mon 7 Jun: Wave 2 go-live, NSW.
  6. Tue 22 Jun: business as usual.

What changes

  • Three Hamlink screens→One customer view
  • Personal spreadsheets→A shared pipeline
  • Emailed referrals→Referrals with an owner
  • A shared mailbox→A complaint case with timers
  • Typed case notes→Drafts the agent reviews

About 1,250 people in eight teams: 808 in Wave 1 and 445 in Wave 2.

  • TeamWhat they hearHow they learn

Watch

School holidays. Go-live is clear, but two of the three Wave 1 training weeks fall in the holidays and Easter. Rosters must allow for leave.

First three moves

  1. Pre-load every banker's pipeline and name the other two champions.
  2. Confirm the Docklands blocks and rosters by Fri 12 Feb.
  3. Get the Compass Assist test set in writing by Fri 5 Feb.
