Why and what

Business case

  • Faster resolution
  • Lower cost to serve
  • Lower risk
  • Better customer experience
  • Reliable data

What is being built

  • Service Cloud for contact centre and complaints cases.
  • Financial Services Cloud for customers, accounts, branch servicing and the banker pipeline.
  • Compass Assist to help staff draft notes. A person reviews every draft.

Know what changes in your day, practise it before go-live, and have someone on your floor to ask in the first two weeks.

Five processes that change

  1. Contact centreThree Hamlink screens and retyped notes become one customer view and one case record.
  2. Branch servicingPaper forms and email referrals become a guided flow and a referral with an owner.
  3. Business pipelinePersonal spreadsheets become a shared pipeline, already loaded on day one.
  4. ComplaintsA shared mailbox becomes one case with categories, timers and reporting.
  5. AI assistantCompass Assist is new, and its rules are written before go-live.
