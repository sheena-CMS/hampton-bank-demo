Project Compass

Compass training nominations and feedback

Draft v0.2 | 29 January 2027 | Change lead, Change Made Simple

Managers nominate people for training one at a time, and learners give feedback after each session. Both forms run on this page only and finish with a CSV download.

Changed in v0.2
  • Session choices on the feedback form now include the Docklands early and late blocks, the branch first block in the week of 12 April and the make-up sessions on Wed 14 and Thu 15 April (change C-4). Updated v0.2 from T5
  • Business banker sessions are 90 minutes from 7:30am, and Compass Assist for bankers is a separate session in week 3 after go-live (change C-4). Updated v0.2 from T4
  • The location list shows the wave for each site, so nominations can be sorted into Wave 1 (808 people) and Wave 2 (445 people).

Nothing is sent anywhere

This form runs in your browser only. Answers are held on the page until you download them as a CSV file. In a real project this form runs on Microsoft Forms, and responses land in a sheet that the project team reads.

Compass training nomination form

Built as a test first For managers

Compass training nominations. Use this form to nominate people from your area for Compass training. Nominate one person at a time. Choose Yes at the end to add another. You can add up to five people in one go. Nothing is sent anywhere from this page. Use dummy details only.
Q1 Your name required
Q9 Nobody from my area needs training optional
Q2 Which business units or functions are you nominating for? required

How the questions connect

  1. Q1 identifies the sender. If Q9 is ticked, the form goes straight to the end.
  2. Q2 chooses the areas. Q3 to Q7 collect one nominee. Q8 asks whether to add another.
  3. Q8 Yes goes back to Q3 for another nominee, up to five times. Q8 No goes to Q10 and the end.
