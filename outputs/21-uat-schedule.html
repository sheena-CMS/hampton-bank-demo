UAT schedule

Draft v0.2 | 29 January 2027 | Change lead, Change Made Simple

Eight sessions across five days start UAT on Mon 8 Feb 2027. UAT runs to Fri 12 Mar and sign-off is Wed 17 Mar.

Changed in v0.2 4 updates
  • Louise Chen's complaint category list is now due Fri 5 Feb, moved from Fri 29 Jan, and the Complaints and advocacy session on Wed 10 Feb depends on it. From T5 (changes C-6 and C-7).
  • Business banker session set at 7:30am for 90 minutes before client calls. From T4 (change C-4).
  • The Compass Assist guardrails gate moves from Fri 26 Mar to Fri 9 Apr, so the Risk and compliance session now feeds a gate after UAT closes. From T5 (change C-3).
  • Version raised to v0.2 (change C-7).
WhenMon 8 to Fri 12 Feb 2027

One session per team, at a fixed time for that team. Contact centre sessions are at 10:30am, bankers at 7:30am. All times are AEDT, so Victoria and NSW share the same clock time.

Format30 minutes, then 1 hour

A 30-minute overview of how UAT runs, then one hour of hands-on training in the test environment. Offered by video, with a recording the same day.

Once the session endsEach tester begins their test scripts

A 30-minute debrief each day at 12:30pm. Weeks 2 to 4 are testing and retest. Week 5 is finish, with sign-off on Wed 17 Mar.
