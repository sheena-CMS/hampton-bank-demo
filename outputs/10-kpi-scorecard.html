Reading the ratings

Green

At or better than the target set by the owner.

Amber

Within tolerance. Tolerance to be set by each owner with the target.

Red

Outside tolerance. The measure goes to the weekly status report as an escalation. See weekly status.

The rating is shown in the live tracker. This page is static and lists measures, not results.

Trap: Measuring between Fri 26 Mar and Sun 11 Apr 2027 gives a distorted baseline, because leave and rosters change contact volumes. Take the baseline from January to March data and flag any weeks that fall inside the school holidays.

Rules for this page

  1. The scorecard lists measures, not results. Baselines come before go-live.
  2. Every number comes from a named report, never from memory. No numbers appear until the sources are checked.
  3. Each measure is something the programme can change and ties to one driver.
  4. Direction is stated on every measure: up, down, within target or no worse.
