Training at a glance / Service / Contact centre agent

Contact centre agent

Handle a contact from search to close. Colour key: this page uses the colour of the role Contact centre agent. Amber is a to-be-confirmed item. Red is a trap.

a1 Find the customer

  1. Search by name or phone number and open the matching customer.
  2. Check the accounts, household members and open cases on the view.
  3. Ask the verification questions and tick Verified when the customer passes. If two customers share a name, check the date of birth first.

Learn more: Trailhead: build households and groups

a2 Open a case and choose the category

  1. Select New case from the customer view.
  2. Choose Enquiry, or Complaint if the customer says they want to complain.
  3. Choose the category from the list. Do not type it in the notes.
Trap: A customer who says they are unhappy and wants it recorded is a complaint. Choose Complaint, even if you can fix the problem on the call.

Learn more: Trailhead: case record types and support processes

a3 Use Compass Assist for the reply and the note

  1. Open the Compass Assist panel, read the suggestion and the article it came from, and edit it for this customer.
  2. At wrap-up select Draft note, check every line against what the customer said, then Save.
Trap: Never copy customer details into a prompt outside Salesforce, and never save a draft you have not read.

Learn more: Trailhead: Einstein Service Replies; Trailhead: Einstein Work Summaries for service agents

a4 Resolve, escalate or pass on a complaint

With: Complaints officer

  1. If you have fixed it, set the outcome and close the case.
  2. If not, select Escalate, choose the second-line queue, add one line on what you tried and tell the customer who owns the case.
  3. For a complaint, select Route to complaints. The case joins TBC: complaint queue name and rule, Q-04 and the timer has started.

Learn more: Trailhead: create a case escalation rule; Salesforce: complaints management for financial services

Training at a glance / Service / Complaints officer

Complaints officer

Open, investigate, answer and close a complaint. Colour key: this page uses the colour of the role Complaints officer. Amber is a to-be-confirmed item. Red is a trap.

c1 Open the case, categorise it and check the timers Updated v0.2 from T5

  1. Check the channel and time received, then choose the category: TBC: final complaint category list, due Fri 5 Feb 2027.
  2. Send the acknowledgement before the first timer ends. Limits: TBC: acknowledgement and response time limits.
  3. If the response timer will not be met, select Escalate to manager and give the reason.
Trap: Timers start when the case is created, not when you open it. Check the start time on any case that came in by letter.

Learn more: Developer reference: PublicComplaint object; Trailhead: entitlement management for Lightning Experience

c2 Investigate with tasks

  1. Select New task, choose the person or team and write the question. The reply is attached to the case.
  2. Record your findings in the case notes.

Learn more: Trailhead: set up the Lightning Service Console

c3 Draft the response with Compass Assist Updated v0.2 from T5

With: Everyone who uses Compass Assist

  1. Select Draft response. Compass Assist drafts from the case and labels it Draft.
  2. Read it against your findings and rewrite anything wrong or unclear.
  3. Remove the Draft label only when you approve the text.
Trap: Compass Assist may draft the response, but you must edit and send it. Never send a draft you have not edited.

Worth knowing: Answered Thu 28 Jan 2027: drafts are allowed, the complaints officer edits and sends, and drafts are labelled.

Learn more: Trailhead: Einstein Work Summaries for service agents

c4 Send the response and close the case

  1. Send the response from the case. A copy is stored on the case.
  2. Choose the outcome, remedy and cause from the lists, then close the case. The closing letter is produced from these fields.

Learn more: Developer reference: PublicComplaint object

  1. Open the team dashboard and check queue length, longest wait and repeat contacts.

Training at a glance / Sales and branch / Branch teller or adviser

Branch teller or adviser

Serve, onboard and refer. Colour key: this page uses the colour of the role Branch teller or adviser. Amber is a to-be-confirmed item. Red is a trap.

b1 Find the customer and update details

  1. Search by name, phone or account reference, open the customer and check the Household panel.
  2. Confirm identity using the branch checklist.
  3. To change an address or phone number, select Edit details. For a change of name, start the Operations task and attach the document.

Learn more: Trailhead: build households and groups

b2 Run the guided onboarding

  1. Select New customer or Open account and follow the screens. Each required field must be filled before Next.
  2. Tick each identity check as you complete it. You can save and return from My open items.
Trap: Do not attach a scan of the customer's ID to the Salesforce record. Put it in TBC: document store for ID images and enter the reference.

Learn more: Trailhead: set up Flow for Service

b3 Refer to a banker and check open referrals

With: Team leader

  1. Open the customer, select New referral, choose the banker or team and write the need in one sentence. Save.
  2. Open the Branch dashboard, check Open referrals and Incomplete onboardings, and open anything older than the target age. At month end, work from the exceptions list.
Trap: Do not also send an email. The referral is the record and a second message creates two versions.

Learn more: Salesforce Help: referral record types (Financial Services Cloud); Trailhead: learn about reports and dashboards

Training at a glance / Sales and branch / Business banker

Business banker

Work the pipeline and answer referrals. Colour key: this page uses the colour of the role Business banker. Amber is a to-be-confirmed item. Red is a trap.

k1 Check your pre-loaded pipeline Updated v0.2 from T4

  1. Open My pipeline. Your deals were loaded from your spreadsheet before this session.
  2. Compare ten deals against your spreadsheet and note any missing or wrong ones on the check sheet.
  3. Tell the trainer before the session ends. The CRM platform team corrects the load.

Worth knowing: The 90-minute session starts at 7:30am so you finish before client calls.

Learn more: Trailhead: import and export with data management tools

k2 Open an opportunity and move it along the stage path

  1. Open the lead or referral, select Convert and fill in the account, amount and expected close date.
  2. Select the next stage and Mark Stage as Complete. For credit, link the documents and move the stage to Credit submission.
  3. The credit team records the decision, not you.

Learn more: Trailhead: work with an opportunity record

k3 Add a client note Updated v0.2 from T4

  1. Open the client, select New note and write what was agreed and any follow-up. Tag the deal.
  2. For older notes from your spreadsheet: TBC: whether spreadsheet notes are loaded, Q-07. Until it is decided, keep them where they are.

Learn more: Trailhead: work with an opportunity record

k4 Opt in to Compass Assist Updated v0.2 from T4

With: Everyone who uses Compass Assist

  1. Attend the short Compass Assist session and switch it on in your settings.
  2. Use it to draft a meeting note or follow-up email. Edit it, then save or send.
Trap: Compass Assist is off for you at go-live. Do not ask a colleague to use it for you. Read every draft before you send it.

Learn more: Trailhead: promote responsible and ethical agents

Training at a glance / AI assistant / Compass Assist rules

Compass Assist rules

Rules for everyone who uses the assistant. Colour key: this page uses the colour of the role Everyone who uses Compass Assist. Amber is a to-be-confirmed item. Red is a trap.

x1 The five Compass Assist rules

  1. Read every suggestion or draft before you save or send it, and edit it so it is correct for this customer.
  2. Never put customer personal data into a prompt outside Salesforce.
  3. Leave the Draft label on until you approve the text.
  4. Report a wrong suggestion to your team leader.
Trap: The rules apply to every user. A draft that looks right can still be wrong.

Learn more: Trailhead: promote responsible and ethical agents

x2 Review before saving and keep personal data inside Salesforce

  1. Read the whole draft, check names, amounts and dates against the case, and save only when you would put your name to it.
  2. Use Compass Assist inside the case so it reads the record itself. Do not paste a customer's details into any other tool.
Trap: Pasting an account number into a chat tool breaks the approval conditions even if the answer is right.

Learn more: Trailhead: establish trust with autonomous agents; Trailhead: meet the Einstein Trust Layer

x3 Report a wrong suggestion

  1. Mark the suggestion Not helpful and note the case number.
  2. Tell your team leader, who adds it to the problem log.

Learn more: Trailhead: Einstein Service Replies

Risk and compliance reviewer

Approve the guardrails and sample use. Colour key: this page uses the colour of the role Risk and compliance reviewer. Amber is a to-be-confirmed item. Red is a trap.

r1 Read the guardrails and the test set

With: CRM platform admin

  1. Open the guardrails document and the test set from the review folder.
  2. Check each permitted use against the policy: answers, case notes, complaint response drafts.
  3. Record questions for the CRM platform team.

Learn more: Trailhead: promote responsible and ethical agents

r2 Run the approval gate Updated v0.2 from T5

  1. Run the test set in the sandbox and record each result.
  2. Decide: approve, approve with conditions, or do not approve.
  3. Send the signed decision to the program director. The go/no-go checklist uses it.
Trap: Nothing that has not passed the gate may be shown in training. The training demo uses the approved guardrails only.

Worth knowing: The gate was Fri 26 Mar 2027 and moved to Fri 9 Apr 2027, ten days before go-live.

Learn more: Trailhead: establish trust with autonomous agents

r3 Sample drafts and review prompt changes

With: CRM platform admin

  1. Each week in hypercare, read ten case notes and ten complaint drafts that were sent. Record any saved without editing.
  2. For a prompt or template change, run the test set against it and approve or return it in writing. TBC: Owner of prompt and template changes, Q-02 is not yet decided.

Learn more: Trailhead: establish trust with autonomous agents

Learning and development trainer

Prepare and deliver the sessions. Colour key: this page uses the colour of the role Learning and development trainer. Amber is a to-be-confirmed item. Red is a trap.

t1 Prepare the session

  1. Check the group, the room and the sandbox, and that each learner has a sandbox login.
  2. Open the demo script. It uses the approved guardrails only.
Trap: Do not demonstrate any Compass Assist behaviour that the guardrails gate has not approved. If the gate has not passed, show screens only.

Learn more: Trailhead: develop and test using sandboxes

t2 Run a 90-minute block and teach review before saving Updated v0.2 from T5

With: Everyone who uses Compass Assist

  1. Run 15 minutes of demo, 45 of hands-on practice from the job page, then 30 of questions and the check-out quiz.
  2. State the rule: a person reads and edits every suggestion or draft before it is saved or sent. Show one good and one wrong draft.
Trap: Do not let learners practise with real customer details. Use the sandbox records only.

Worth knowing: Wave 1 blocks for Docklands run early (6:30am to 8:00am) and late (4:30pm to 6:00pm). Business bankers train at 7:30am for 90 minutes. Branches have a first block in the week of 12 Apr and make-up sessions on Wed 14 and Thu 15 Apr.
