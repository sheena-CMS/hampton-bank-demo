Training schedule

Draft v0.2 | 29 January 2027 | Change lead, Change Made Simple

Wave 1 training runs Tue 30 Mar to Fri 16 Apr 2027 and Wave 2 runs Mon 17 May to Fri 4 Jun. The Victorian school holidays and Easter sit inside the first two weeks of Wave 1.

Changed in v0.2 4 updates
  • Docklands gets early (6:30 to 8am) and late (4:30 to 6pm) 90-minute blocks. From T5 (change C-4).
  • Branches get a first block in the week of 12 Apr for people on leave, and make-up sessions on Wed 14 and Thu 15 Apr. From T5 (change C-4).
  • Business bankers move to 90-minute sessions at 7:30am, after their pipeline is pre-loaded, and Compass Assist training moves to week 3 after go-live as opt-in. From T4 (change C-4).
  • The Compass Assist guardrails gate moves from Fri 26 Mar to Fri 9 Apr, so every Compass Assist block is scheduled after it. From T5 (change C-3). Version raised to v0.2 (change C-7).

Training is by role and starts after UAT sign-off. It is booked in the bank's learning system and completion is read from it. Each wave has three weeks of training before go-live and two weeks of hypercare after.

Trap: the Victorian school holidays (Fri 26 Mar to Sun 11 Apr 2027) and Easter (Good Friday Fri 26 Mar, Easter Monday Mon 29 Mar) cover the first two of the three weeks of Wave 1 training. Staff on leave and parents on rosters cannot attend, and contact centre shifts have less cover. Term 2 starts Mon 12 Apr, a week before go-live. Dates are taken from Victorian school term dates and holidays and Victorian public holidays 2027. Nothing is scheduled on Good Friday or Easter Monday. Anzac Day falls on Sun 25 Apr, so it does not move any session.

How the school-holiday clash is handled Updated v0.2 from T5

The decision at T5 on Thu 28 Jan keeps go-live on Mon 19 Apr and starts training on Tue 30 Mar. Four changes make that work.

Docklands early and late blocks

Blocks run 6:30 to 8am and 4:30 to 6pm so shifts can attend either side of their work. Sam Petrovic confirms the trainer blocks by Fri 12 Feb (action A-26).

Branch first block

People on leave in the first two weeks take their first block in the week of 12 Apr. Branch leaders list them at the roster check in March.

Make-up sessions

Wed 14 and Thu 15 Apr are held for anyone who still has a block outstanding. They are the last sessions before the completion check on Fri 16 Apr.

Bankers after the holidays

Bankers train Mon 12 to Fri 16 Apr at 7:30am, after the pre-load, so no banker session depends on holiday cover.

Alternative considered and rejected: move Victorian go-live to Mon 3 May

Moving go-live two weeks would put all three weeks of training after the school holidays, from Tue 13 Apr to Fri 30 Apr. It was rejected at T5 for three reasons.

  1. It pushes Victorian hypercare to Fri 14 May, so it overlaps the start of Wave 2 training on Mon 17 May. The eight L&D staff deliver all training and would carry both (risk R-04).
  2. It gives the sponsor less time to show a customer experience result by June. Helen Marchetti asked for a visible win by then.
  3. It leaves a go-live on Mon 3 May followed by NSW go-live on Mon 7 Jun, only five weeks apart, with no gap for fixes between waves.

How training is delivered

Role-based sessions

Sessions run as 90-minute blocks of up to 25 people, allocated by Salesforce role and booked by the person. Wave 1 needs 36 cohorts and Wave 2 needs 20.

Platform: the bank's learning system and Teams.

Modules and guides

Each role has a one-page guide for each task it uses, and every session is recorded for people on leave. Nobody is trained on a function they will not use.

Platform: the Project Compass intranet page.

Drop-ins and walk-rounds

Virtual drop-ins run in each hypercare fortnight, and team leaders and champions walk their floors. None are scheduled from Fri 26 Mar to Sun 11 Apr.

Platform: Teams, and on site at Docklands, Parramatta and branches.

Vendor training

Northgate Digital trains the L&D team (Mon 22 to Thu 25 Mar) and the CRM platform team (Tue 30 Mar to Thu 1 Apr). Jorge Alvarez leads both.

Platform: the test environment.

The gate

Training completion is at least 90 percent in each wave group before go-live. That is criterion 12 of 16 on the go / no-go checklist, read on Fri 16 Apr for Wave 1 and Fri 4 Jun for Wave 2. Compass Assist blocks run only after the guardrails gate on Fri 9 Apr Updated v0.2.
