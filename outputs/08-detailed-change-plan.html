Detailed change plan

Draft v0.2 | 29 January 2027 | Change lead, Change Made Simple

What is changing at Hampton Bank, who is affected, and how the change is managed to handover on 22 June 2027, across Victoria in April and New South Wales in June.

Changed in v0.2 6 updates
  • Training: Wave 1 works around the school holidays and Easter, with early and late Docklands blocks and make-up sessions on 14 and 15 April. From T5.
  • Business bankers: 90-minute 7:30am sessions, pipelines pre-loaded, Compass Assist opt-in from week 3. From T4.
  • Guardrails gate: Risk and Compliance sign-off moves from Fri 26 Mar to Fri 9 Apr, ten days before go-live. From T5.
  • Stakeholders: Richard Vance moves from Resistant to Conditional, and three banker champions are added. From T4.
  • Risks: R-03 rises to High, R-01 mitigation is rewritten, and R-13 and R-14 are new. Sources T4 and T5.
  • Complaint drafting: Q-05 is now fact. Compass Assist may draft complaint responses; the complaints officer edits and sends them.

Hampton Bank is replacing its legacy CRM, Hamlink, with Salesforce, and adding an AI assistant called Compass Assist to help staff draft notes. About 1,250 people change how they work, in two waves: Victoria first, then New South Wales.

What to watch

Go-live on Mon 19 Apr 2027 is clear of the school holidays, but the first two of the three Wave 1 training weeks fall inside the holidays and Easter (Fri 26 Mar to Sun 11 Apr).
