Detailed change plan

Hampton Bank is replacing its legacy CRM, Hamlink, with Salesforce, and adding an AI assistant called Compass Assist to help staff draft notes. About 1,250 people change how they work, in two waves: Victoria first, then New South Wales.

Overall change level
ContainedSignificantComplexTransformational

Eight teams in two states, five processes, and an AI assistant that is new to everyone.

Expert insight

Adoption is most at risk in business banking. Bankers will not give up their spreadsheets unless the pipeline view is better on day one, so the pre-load matters more than the training.

First three moves

  1. Pre-load every banker's pipeline and name the other two champions (A-24, A-25).
  2. Confirm the early and late Docklands blocks and the rosters by Fri 12 Feb (A-26).
  3. Get the Compass Assist test set and gate date in writing by Fri 5 Feb (A-27).

Important warning: the head of business banking is Conditional, not yet supportive, and two of the three Wave 1 training weeks fall in the school holidays and Easter. Since December 2025, poor change management is also a named psychosocial hazard under Australian work health and safety law, so listening comes before each rollout step.

Know what changes in your day, practise it before go-live, and have someone on your floor to ask in the first two weeks.

What is being built, which processes change, and how hard the change lands on each group.

Solution design

Service Cloud

Contact centre and complaints cases.

Financial Services Cloud

Customers, accounts, branch servicing and the banker pipeline.

Compass Assist

Helps staff draft notes. A person reviews every draft, and it is off for bankers at go-live.

Detail: Solution overview.

Processes that change

  • Case handling (High) and branch servicing (Medium): one customer view, guided onboarding, referrals with an owner.
  • Banker pipeline (High): a shared pipeline, pre-loaded from each banker's spreadsheet.
  • Complaints (High): a case type with categories, timers and reporting.
  • AI assistant (Medium): suggested answers and drafted notes, always reviewed.

Contact centre

350 people, Docklands and Parramatta | Leader: Tom Gallagher Supportive, worried about shift cover
How they work: frontline, on shifts

Impact
Three Hamlink screens and retyped notes become one customer view and one case record. Compass Assist drafts the notes.
Listening
Team leaders ask at shift huddles what worries agents most and pass it to the Change lead each week.
Engagement
Huddles and roster briefings from team leaders, backed by the intranet and email. Tom Gallagher fronts the shift-cover plan.
Key message
The three-screen workflow is now one screen, and training comes in 90-minute blocks that fit your shift.
Capability
90-minute blocks across all shifts, with early (6:30am) and late (4:30pm) blocks at Docklands.

Branch network

600 people in 60 branches | Leader: Aroha Williams Supportive
How they work: frontline, across many sites

Impact
Paper forms and emailed referrals become guided onboarding and a referral with an owner.
Listening
Branch managers collect questions at branch huddles. TBC: NSW champions are not yet named.
Engagement
Branch managers carry one consistent message, with a school-holiday roster reminder before Fri 26 Mar.
Key message
Referrals now live in the system with an owner, and training is planned around month end and Easter trading.
Capability
Standard blocks, a first block in the week of 12 April, and make-ups on Wed 14 and Thu 15 Apr. TBC: Q-03 at the branch or on site.

Business bankers

80 people, 45 in Victoria and 35 in NSW | Leader: Richard Vance Conditional, was Resistant
How they work: client-facing, each with a personal pipeline

Impact
Personal spreadsheets give way to a shared pipeline.
Listening
Through the three champions rather than the reporting line. Isla Fraser, Dev and Karina hear concerns first.
Engagement
Messages come from the champions and the Change lead. Richard Vance engages once the pipeline view is better than the spreadsheets.
Key message
Your pipeline is already loaded when you first log in, and Compass Assist is your choice from week 3.
Capability
90-minute sessions at 7:30am, before client calls. Pipelines pre-loaded for at least 90 percent of bankers by Fri 9 Apr.

Complaints and advocacy

40 people at head office | Leader: Louise Chen Supportive
How they work: office-based, to regulated time limits

Impact
A shared mailbox and Hamlink notes become one complaint case with categories, timers and reporting.
Listening
The team checks the category mapping before UAT, so concerns surface in testing and not after go-live.
Engagement
Direct from Louise Chen in team meetings.
Key message
Your categories and timers are mapped before UAT, and Compass Assist drafts are labelled and yours to edit.
Capability
A four-step job page, with practice in the sandbox on masked records.
